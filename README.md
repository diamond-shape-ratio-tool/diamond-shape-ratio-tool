# Diamond Shape Ratio Calculator – Diamond Proportion Analysis Tool

## Project Overview

Diamond Shape Ratio Calculator is a web-based educational application designed to help users calculate and explore the length-to-width ratios of different diamond shapes.

Developed as an educational resource by Laxmi Jewellers Dubai, the tool makes it easier to understand diamond proportions through simple measurements and instant calculations.

## Key Features

- **Accurate Ratio Calculations:** Enter diamond length and width in millimetres to calculate the length-to-width ratio.
- **Multiple Diamond Shapes:** Explore oval, pear, marquise, emerald, radiant and other fancy-cut diamond shapes.
- **Instant Results:** Get proportion calculations directly in your browser.
- **Educational Guidance:** Understand how different ratios influence a diamond's visual appearance.
- **Simple Interface:** Easy-to-use functionality suitable for beginners and jewellery professionals.
- **Browser Compatibility:** Access the calculator without installing additional software or registering an account.

## Who Can Use This Tool?

The calculator is designed for jewellery buyers, gemmology students, diamond enthusiasts, jewellery designers and developers interested in diamond measurements and proportions.

## How It Works

Enter the diamond's length and width in millimetres. The calculator divides the length by the width to produce a numerical ratio, helping users understand whether a diamond appears relatively elongated or closer to a square proportion.

## Educational Purpose

Length-to-width ratio describes a diamond's proportions but does not determine its overall quality. Diamond appearance and performance also depend on factors such as cut precision, symmetry, light behaviour and individual preferences.
